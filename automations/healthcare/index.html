    Healthcare Automations

    53 done-for-you automations built for the roles that keep this industry running. Book a consultation and we'll tailor any of these to your exact tools.

    Medical Office Manager

    Front Desk Daily Briefing & Schedule Gap Alert

    Each morning before the office opens, the office manager and front desk team receive an automated briefing: today's confirmed appointments, patients who haven't confirmed, the day's unscheduled gaps, and any outstanding patient balances over a set threshold. Unconfirmed appointments trigger an automated text asking the patient to confirm or reschedule. Schedule gaps that appear during the day are automatically pushed to a waitlist outreach sequence so that cancellations are filled before the slot goes empty, and the front desk team starts every single day fully informed rather than discovering problems as they arrive.

    Tools Required
    Practice management software (e.g., Kareo or SimplePractice)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (confirmation texts)Email or Slack (morning briefing)Google Sheets (gap and waitlist tracker)
    Patient Care Coordinator

    Referral Tracking & Specialist Follow-Up System

    When a provider refers a patient to a specialist, this automation logs the referral with the patient name, specialist, diagnosis code, and urgency level, sends the referral packet to the specialist office automatically, and texts the patient their specialist's contact information with instructions to schedule. If confirmation that the appointment was booked hasn't been received within seven days, a follow-up task is created and the patient receives another reminder, closing the referral loop that typically gets lost the moment the patient walks out the door.

    Tools Required
    EHR with API access (e.g., athenahealth or eClinicalWorks)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (patient reminder texts)Airtable or Google Sheets (referral tracking log)Email (specialist packet delivery)
    Medical Billing Specialist

    Eligibility Verification & Prior Authorization Tracker

    For every appointment scheduled more than 48 hours out, this automation automatically verifies the patient's insurance eligibility and pulls their current deductible and copay information, flagging any coverage discrepancies before the patient arrives. Procedures that require prior authorization are identified from the appointment type, a pre-auth request is initiated automatically, and the status is tracked daily until approved, ensuring no one discovers a missing auth after a procedure has already been performed, which is when it becomes a write-off instead of a payment.

    Tools Required
    Clearinghouse with eligibility API (e.g., Availity or Change Healthcare)Practice management softwareZapier or Make.comGoogle Sheets or Airtable (pre-auth tracker)Slack or email (eligibility flag alerts)
    Practice Administrator

    Staff Credentialing & License Expiration Tracker

    Every provider and licensed staff member's credentials (DEA license, state medical license, malpractice insurance, BLS certification, and board certifications) are tracked with expiration dates in a central system. Renewal reminders are sent to the staff member at 120, 60, and 30 days before expiration, and the practice administrator receives a weekly compliance dashboard showing every credential status across the entire practice. Any credential that lapses without a renewal on file triggers an immediate escalation, because an expired license is a compliance and liability issue that cannot wait until the next Friday report.

    Tools Required
    Airtable or credentialing software (credential database)Zapier or Make.comEmail or Slack (staff renewal reminders and admin escalations)Jotform (renewal document upload portal)Google Sheets (compliance dashboard)

    Scheduling Coordinator

    Cancellation Waitlist Fill System

    The moment an appointment is canceled, this automation checks the waitlist for that provider and appointment type, ranks patients by how long they've been waiting and their stated availability, and texts the top match with the open slot and a 10-minute window to claim it with a single reply. If there's no response in 10 minutes, the offer automatically cascades to the next patient on the list, continuing until the slot is filled or the list is exhausted. The scheduling coordinator sees the entire cascade and final outcome logged in one place, with no manual calling down a paper list required. Practices running this typically fill same-day cancellations that used to sit open for the rest of the day within 20 minutes of the cancellation itself.
