    Restaurants & Food Service Automations

    53 done-for-you automations built for the roles that keep this industry running. Book a consultation and we'll tailor any of these to your exact tools.

    Restaurant Owner

    Online Review Response & Reputation Management System

    Every time a new review is posted on Google, Yelp, or TripAdvisor, the system flags it, classifies the sentiment, and drafts a personalized response for the owner to approve and post, with negative reviews routed immediately to a priority inbox rather than sitting unnoticed for days. Over time, the system tracks rating trends and identifies the most common complaint themes so the owner can address root causes rather than just managing one bad review at a time.

    Tools Required
    Zapier or Make.comGoogle Business Profile APIYelp Fusion API (monitoring)OpenAI API (draft responses)Slack or email (negative review alerts)
    Restaurant Manager

    Staff Scheduling & Open-Shift Fill System

    When a team member calls out or a shift goes uncovered, the system automatically texts qualified, available staff in seniority order until someone confirms coverage, then updates the schedule in real time and notifies the manager. Scheduling for the upcoming week is drafted automatically based on historical sales volume by day and time, so the manager starts from a smart baseline rather than a blank spreadsheet every week.

    Tools Required
    Scheduling software (e.g., 7shifts or When I Work)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS shift alerts)Slack (manager notifications)
    Catering & Events Manager

    Event Inquiry & Banquet Event Order (BEO) Generator

    Catering inquiries submitted via the website are automatically qualified (guest count, date, event type, budget) and the system generates a preliminary quote and schedules a discovery call on the events manager's calendar. Once the event is confirmed and details are finalized, the BEO document is automatically assembled from the collected information and sent to the client for review and signature, eliminating hours of back-and-forth and manual document formatting.

    Tools Required
    Jotform or Typeform (inquiry form)Zapier or Make.comCalendly (discovery call scheduling)PandaDoc or DocuSign (BEO generation)Google Sheets (event tracker)
    Bar Manager

    Inventory Count & Par-Level Reorder System

    Weekly inventory counts submitted by bar staff through a simple mobile form are automatically compared against par levels set for each product, and any item below threshold generates a purchase order to the appropriate distributor or supplier. The bar manager gets a single consolidated order sheet rather than rebuilding it from scratch every week, and the system tracks cost-per-pour trends over time to flag when a product's margin is slipping before it shows up in the P&L.

    Tools Required
    Jotform or Google Forms (inventory count form)Zapier or Make.comGoogle Sheets or Airtable (par level database)Email (auto-generated purchase orders)
    Food Truck Owner

    Daily Location Announcement & Pre-Order System

    Each morning, the owner confirms the day's location in a simple form, and the system automatically posts it to Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business, sends a text blast to the VIP customer list, and opens a pre-order window for pickup so customers can order ahead from wherever they are. The result is a ready line of customers when the truck arrives, higher per-location revenue, and a growing follower base that never has to wonder where the truck is today.

    Tools Required
    Zapier or Make.comMeta Business Suite (Facebook/Instagram)Twilio (SMS list)Square or Toast (pre-order integration)Google Business Profile API
    Reservations Manager

    Reservation Confirmation & No-Show Reducer

    The moment a guest books a table online or by phone, the system logs it in the reservation platform and immediately sends a text confirmation with the date, time, and party size. A reminder text goes out 24 hours before the reservation asking the guest to reply "C" to confirm or "X" to cancel, and a second, shorter reminder fires two hours before the seating window for same-day bookings. Guests who don't respond to the 24-hour reminder are automatically called by a staff member the next morning instead of being left to guess, and no-show rates that used to run near 15-20% on weekend nights drop into the single digits once guests know a real person is tracking whether they show up.

    Tools Required
    OpenTable or ResyZapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS confirmations and reminders)Google Sheets (no-show tracking)
    Host Stand Lead

    Text-Based Waitlist & Table-Ready Alerts

    Guests join the waitlist at the host stand or by scanning a QR code from the sidewalk, entering their name, party size, and phone number instead of waiting for a buzzer or straining to hear their name called over restaurant noise. As tables turn, the host taps "next" and the guest gets an automatic text letting them know their table is ready, with a second text sent if they don't check in within five minutes. Wait time estimates update automatically based on how quickly parties ahead of them are actually being seated, not a fixed 20-minute guess, so guests can step away instead of crowding the entryway, and hosts spend far less time answering "how much longer" every ninety seconds.

    Tools Required
    Waitlist platform (e.g., Yelp Waitlist or NoWait)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS)POS system (e.g., Toast) for table status
    General Manager

    Post-Visit Review Funnel & Feedback Router

    When the POS detects a closed check, the system waits two hours, then sends the guest a text or email asking one simple question: how was your visit? Guests who respond positively are automatically routed to a direct link to leave a Google or Yelp review, while guests who indicate anything less than great are routed instead to a private feedback form that lands directly in the general manager's inbox rather than a public review page. The manager can respond to a dissatisfied guest the same day, often before they've even thought about posting anywhere, and the restaurant's public rating climbs because only the genuinely happy visits get funneled toward public platforms.

    Tools Required
    POS system (e.g., Toast or Square)Zapier or Make.comTwilio or Mailchimp (outreach)Google Business Profile and Yelp Fusion API (review links)Jotform (private feedback form)
    Catering Sales Coordinator

    Instant Catering Inquiry Auto-Quote System

    A catering inquiry submitted through the website form, guest count, event date, menu preferences, is instantly parsed and matched against a standing per-head pricing sheet, generating a starter quote with two or three available date options sent back to the prospect within minutes instead of the next business day. The coordinator is notified immediately with the full inquiry details and a draft quote already attached, ready to refine rather than build from scratch. Catering leads often request quotes from three competitors at once, so being the first to respond with real numbers, sometimes within five minutes of submission, has meaningfully increased the percentage of inquiries that turn into booked events.

    Tools Required

    Marketing Coordinator

    Slow-Night Demand Trigger & Promo Blast

    The system checks reservation volume for each upcoming night against a rolling average for that day of the week, and when bookings for, say, a Tuesday fall more than 30% below normal with less than 48 hours to go, it automatically triggers a promo, a discount code or a limited-time offer, pushed out as an email and text to the restaurant's guest list. The offer is capped at a set number of redemptions so it doesn't cannibalize a night that ends up busy anyway, and results (redemptions, covers seated, revenue) are logged automatically so the coordinator can see which offers actually fill seats. Slow Tuesdays that used to just be accepted as slow now regularly close within 10-15% of a normal night's covers.

    Tools Required
    Reservation platform (e.g., OpenTable or Resy)Zapier or Make.comMailchimp and Twilio (email/SMS blast)Airtable (guest list database)

    Birthday Club Auto-Offer System

Guests who don't redeem within the first two weeks get a friendly reminder text with the offer attached again. A free item redeemed almost always comes attached to a full paying party, so the birthday club consistently drives visits that wouldn't have happened that month otherwise, turning a single data field collected at checkout into a recurring, low-cost source of return traffic.

    Tools Required

The kitchen manager gets a same-day notification of exactly what was ordered and from whom, and a weekly variance report flags ingredients being used faster than recipes predict, a signal of over-portioning or waste. What used to be a Sunday-night ritual of counting walk-in shelves and phoning three different suppliers is now confirmed automatically before the manager finishes prep for the day.
