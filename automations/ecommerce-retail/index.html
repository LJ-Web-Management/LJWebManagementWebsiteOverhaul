    Store Owner

    Post-Purchase Cross-Sell & Review Request Sequence

    Seven days after a customer's first order is delivered, they automatically receive a product-specific email recommending complementary items based on what they bought, a curated cross-sell rather than a generic "you might also like" blast. Fourteen days after delivery, a review request goes out with a direct link to the product page, and customers who leave a review receive a thank-you discount code for their next order. The entire post-purchase relationship is managed automatically, turning a one-time transaction into a repeat customer without the store owner building a single manual campaign.

    Customer Service Manager

    Proactive Order Delay Notification & Resolution System

    When a fulfillment delay is detected (a carrier scan gap, an out-of-stock item, or a warehouse hold) this automation identifies every affected order, sends each customer a proactive update before they contact support, and offers a resolution option: wait with a discount code, cancel, or switch to an in-stock alternative. Customers who are notified proactively generate far fewer inbound support tickets and have significantly higher resolution rates than those who discovered the delay themselves, turning an operational problem into a customer loyalty moment.

    Tools Required
    Shopify or WooCommerce (order data)Shipping carrier API or EasyPost (delay detection)Zapier or Make.comKlaviyo or email (proactive notification)Gorgias (case creation for escalations)
    Inventory Manager

    Supplier Lead Time Tracker & Safety Stock Calculator

    As purchase orders are placed and received, this automation tracks the actual lead time for each supplier and SKU, not the lead time the supplier promised, but the lead time they delivered. Over a rolling 90-day window, it calculates average and worst-case lead times per supplier and adjusts the recommended safety stock level accordingly. When a supplier's reliability score drops below a set threshold, the inventory manager receives an alert to either adjust reorder points or begin qualifying an alternative supplier, before a reliability issue causes a stockout that was entirely predictable in hindsight.

    Tools Required
    Inventory management platform (e.g., Cin7, Skubana, or Linnworks)Zapier or Make.comGoogle Sheets (lead time and safety stock model)Email or Slack (supplier reliability alerts)Airtable (supplier scorecards)
    Marketing Manager

    Influencer & UGC Content Collection & Publishing Pipeline

    When a customer uses the brand's hashtag, tags the brand in a post, or is identified as a micro-influencer in the product niche, this automation flags the content for the marketing manager to review, sends a templated DM requesting usage rights, and (once approved) adds the asset to a shared content library with tags for product, platform, and visual style. Approved UGC is automatically scheduled into the social calendar to fill planned content slots, reducing the time spent hunting for authentic content to near zero while keeping the feed consistently fresh.

    Tools Required
    Social listening tool (e.g., Mention or a hashtag monitoring app)Zapier or Make.comAirtable or Google Sheets (UGC asset library)Buffer or Later (content scheduling)Instagram and TikTok APIs (DM rights request)

What used to be a multi-day back-and-forth email chain becomes a self-service process that handles itself in hours.

Carts recovered through this sequence convert at a meaningfully higher rate than carts left to sit untouched, turning a leaky point in the funnel into a reliable revenue source that runs without anyone monitoring it.
