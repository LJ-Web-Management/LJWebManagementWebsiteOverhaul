    Property Management Automations

    40 done-for-you automations built for the roles that keep this industry running. Book a consultation and we'll tailor any of these to your exact tools.

    Book a Consultation See How It Works
    Maintenance Coordinator

    Maintenance Request Triage & Vendor Dispatch System

    A tenant-submitted maintenance request is automatically categorized by urgency and repair type, then routed to the appropriate vendor with the tenant's photos and description attached, instead of the maintenance coordinator manually reading every ticket and deciding who to call. Plumbing goes to the plumber, electrical to the electrician, and anything flagged urgent jumps straight to the front of the queue with an internal alert, cutting the time between a tenant reporting a problem and a vendor actually being dispatched from days down to minutes.

    Tools Required
    Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS)Jotform (maintenance request form)
    Accounts Receivable Coordinator

    Rent Payment Reminder & Late Fee Notice System

    Tenants automatically receive a reminder a few days before rent is due, and if payment hasn't posted after the grace period, an automatic late notice with the calculated fee is generated and sent, without the accounts receivable coordinator manually running a delinquency report and drafting notices one at a time. Every notice is logged with a timestamp, giving a clean record if the matter ever needs to escalate further, and on-time payment rates climb noticeably once tenants know a reminder, not silence, is what follows a missed due date.

    Tools Required
    Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS)Mailchimp or email
    Leasing Manager

    90/60/30-Day Lease Renewal Countdown Sequence

    Both the tenant and the leasing manager are automatically notified on a fixed schedule, 90, 60, and 30 days out, before a lease expires, so a renewal offer or move-out plan is set in motion with real time to spare instead of the deadline sneaking up on everyone. Tenants who haven't responded to the 60-day notice get a personal follow-up call flagged automatically, and the leasing manager's dashboard shows every unit's renewal status at a glance, replacing a spreadsheet that only ever reflected reality if someone remembered to update it that week.

    Tools Required
    Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS)Mailchimp or email
    Move Coordinator

    Move-In/Move-Out Checklist & Inspection Scheduler

    Based on the move-in or move-out date already on file, tenants automatically receive a digital checklist covering exactly what's expected of them, along with a direct link to schedule the required inspection, instead of the move coordinator emailing each tenant individually and hoping they read it. Inspections that aren't scheduled within a set window automatically trigger a reminder, and both move-ins and move-outs run through the same consistent, documented process every time, which matters most when a deposit dispute later depends on exactly what condition a unit was in on a specific day.

    Tools Required
    Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)Zapier or Make.comCalendly (inspection scheduling)Mailchimp or email
    Marketing Coordinator

    Instant Vacancy Listing Syndication

    The moment a unit is marked available in the property management platform, its listing, photos, price, and availability date included, is automatically pushed out to Zillow, Apartments.com, and every other connected rental portal simultaneously, instead of the marketing coordinator logging into each site one by one to post the same listing manually. Listings that go live across every channel within minutes of a unit turning vacant get more views and more qualified inquiries in the first critical days than listings posted piecemeal over a week, directly shrinking the number of days a unit sits empty and earning nothing.

    Tools Required
    Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)Zapier or Make.comZillow Rental Manager or listing syndication tool
    Leasing Agent

    Rental Application Status Update Sequence

    Prospective tenants automatically receive an update at each stage of the application process, received, screening in progress, approved, or denied, without the leasing agent having to personally call or email every applicant to relay the same news. Applicants left wondering what's happening tend to keep looking elsewhere, so closing that communication gap measurably improves the rate of approved applicants actually signing the lease instead of taking a different unit that responded faster, and the leasing agent's day is freed from a steady stream of "any update" calls.

    Tools Required
    Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS)Mailchimp or email

Vendors who don't confirm within the follow-up window are automatically flagged so the job can be reassigned before a tenant is left waiting on a no-show, and a documented confirmation trail exists for every job the moment it's assigned rather than only existing in someone's memory of a phone call.
