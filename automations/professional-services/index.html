    CPA / Accountant

    Tax Season Document Collection & Deadline Tracker

    At the start of tax season, every client automatically receives a personalized document request list tailored to their entity type, with a secure upload portal link. The system tracks what has been received versus what is outstanding and sends weekly nudge emails and texts to clients with missing items, escalating frequency as the filing deadline approaches. The accountant sees a live dashboard showing which returns are ready to work, which are waiting on client documents, and which are at risk of missing the deadline, replacing the sticky-note system that usually passes for project management during tax season.

    Tools Required
    Client portal (e.g., TaxDome, Canopy, or Karbon)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (client document reminders)Google Sheets or Airtable (return status dashboard)
    Business Coach

    Client Onboarding & Session Prep Automation

    New coaching clients are automatically sent a welcome packet, a discovery questionnaire, a pre-work assignment, and a self-scheduling link for their first session, all within minutes of signing their agreement. Before every session, both the coach and the client receive a prep reminder with a link to the shared session notes document and a brief agenda form for the client to fill out. After each session, the client receives a summary of their commitments and a check-in question for 72 hours later, so accountability is built into the program structure rather than depending on the client to remember what they said they would do.

    Tools Required
    Coaching platform (e.g., Practice or HoneyBook)Jotform (discovery questionnaire and session prep form)Zapier or Make.comCalendly (self-scheduling)Notion or Google Docs (shared session notes)Twilio (72-hour accountability check-in)
