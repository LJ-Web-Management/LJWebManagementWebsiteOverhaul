    Automotive Automations

    51 done-for-you automations built for the roles that keep this industry running. Book a consultation and we'll tailor any of these to your exact tools.

    Dealership Sales Manager

    Lead Follow-Up & Test Drive Scheduling System

    Every new internet lead (from the website, third-party listing sites, or ad campaigns) is immediately acknowledged with a personalized text and email, entered into the CRM with a lead score, and assigned to the first available salesperson. If the salesperson hasn't made contact within two hours, the manager receives an escalation alert. Leads who engage are guided straight to a self-scheduling link for a test drive appointment, cutting the average time from inquiry to appointment and dramatically increasing the percentage of leads that ever reach the lot.

    Tools Required
    CRM (e.g., VinSolutions or DealerSocket)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (immediate lead text)Calendly (test drive scheduling)Slack (escalation alerts)
    Service Advisor

    Repair Order Status & Vehicle Ready Notification System

    From the moment a vehicle is dropped off, the customer receives automated status updates at each stage of the repair process (vehicle received, inspection complete with recommended services, repair in progress, and vehicle ready for pickup) all via text without the service advisor having to make a single phone call. Vehicles that have been ready for more than four hours but haven't been picked up trigger a reminder text to the customer and a flag to the service advisor, keeping the service bay from becoming a parking lot.

    Tools Required
    DMS (e.g., CDK Global or Reynolds & Reynolds)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS status updates)Slack (internal flags for long-sitting vehicles)
    Auto Repair Shop Owner

    Maintenance Reminder & Lapsed Customer Win-Back System

    Based on the mileage and service interval recorded at each visit, this automation calculates when each customer is due for their next oil change, tire rotation, or recommended service and sends a reminder text two weeks before that estimated date. Customers who haven't returned in six months receive a win-back offer automatically, and any customer who leaves a negative review online is immediately flagged for a personal call from the owner. The shop's relationship with every customer is actively managed even when the shop is buried in cars.

    Tools Required
    Shop management software (e.g., Shop-Ware or Tekmetric)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (maintenance reminder SMS)Mailchimp (email campaigns)Google Business Profile API (review monitoring)
    Finance & Insurance Manager

    Deal Paperwork Checklist & Funding Status Tracker

    The moment a deal is desked, the system generates a complete checklist of every document required for that specific deal (based on the lender, product mix, and customer type) and sends it to the F&I manager's deal queue. As documents are received and uploaded, the checklist updates in real time, and the F&I manager knows exactly what's missing before they sit down with the customer. After signing, the system tracks funding status with each lender and alerts the accounting office the moment a deal is funded and ready to post.

    Fleet Sales Representative

    Fleet Renewal & Expansion Campaign System

    Twelve months before the end of a fleet account's vehicle replacement cycle, the system automatically triggers an outreach sequence from the fleet rep, a summary of the account's current vehicles, a preview of upcoming model changes, and a link to schedule a renewal consultation. Fleet managers who engage receive a custom proposal generated from their account data; those who don't respond are re-queued for a phone call. Between cycles, the fleet rep receives alerts when a tracked account adds employees, expands locations, or posts about growth, signals that a fleet expansion conversation is timely.

    Tools Required

Shops that used to rely on customers remembering to call back in for their 5,000-mile service now see a steady, predictable stream of return visits instead of hoping people show up on their own.

    Warranty Administrator

    VIN Recall Cross-Check & Notification

    Once a week, this automation runs every customer VIN in the shop's database against the NHTSA recall API and manufacturer recall feeds, flagging any vehicle with an open, unaddressed recall. Affected customers are automatically sent a text and email explaining the specific recall in plain language, along with a link to book the repair, which for many recalls is performed at no cost to the customer. What used to require someone manually checking VINs one at a time, or customers finding out about a recall only after an incident, now runs as a standing weekly check that keeps the shop ahead of safety issues and turns recall traffic into an opportunity to build trust instead of just processing a mandatory fix.
